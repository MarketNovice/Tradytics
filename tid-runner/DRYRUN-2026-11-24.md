## Observations

- **Bars pagination:** `/stocks/bars` returns the newest `limit` bars first, with `next_cursor` pointing to older bars. Past 100 sessions the runner pages correctly (no silent truncation), but each symbol costs 2+ calls, so a rolling start date is still worth adding.
- **Same-day bar revision:** SPY 2026-11-24 changed between two fetches 7 minutes apart, well after the close (20:25 to 20:32 ET): volume 53.5M to 64.7M, close 718.79 to 718.78. The runner's 16:30 ET "complete" cutoff is too early; the 06:00 ET morning capture is the right first capture for the prior session, and evening values should not be treated as final.
- **Flow sign convention:** `net_premium_usd = call_premium_usd - put_premium_usd`, and both components are signed (for example RIOT 11-24: calls +0.61M, puts -4.64M, net +5.25M; NVDA 09-25: calls -11.41M, puts -3.76M, net -7.65M). Negative put premium appears to mean bearish-side put flow was net sold, i.e. bullish. Confirm against vendor docs before writing protocol §2.
- **Premium and delta can disagree:** RIOT 11-24 net premium +5.25M but net delta -11.25M. §2 must pick one measure.
- **Missing flow days are omitted, not zero:** CMI has 23 of 43 sessions, EXE 34, VSAT 36, GPN 37. A missing day cannot be distinguished from a capture gap without a separate completeness check.
- **Daily flow is aggregate only:** fields are call/put/net premium, net delta, put/call ratio, prints, price. No per-print premium, DTE or side, so §2's print-level definitions need either a prints feed or a rewrite in terms of these aggregates.
- **Quotes include after-hours:** `session: "after_hours"` with a separate `regular_close`.
- **Session date:** the run used ET date 2026-11-24 (UTC was already 11-25). Correct.
